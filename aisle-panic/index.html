HOW TO PLAY

DRAG to move your aircraft. Your lasers fire automatically.

SHOOT the nine Alien Supermarket products for points and combos.

AVOID BOMBS. Touching or shooting a bomb costs a life.

You start with 3 lives. Rare falling hearts restore one life if you catch them (maximum 5). When your lives run out, you restart at Aisle 1.

SPECIAL AISLES: Grocery Rush, Bonus Aisles, and Bomb Rain mix up the action. Clear an ordinary aisle without damage or shooting a bomb for a Perfect Aisle bonus.
