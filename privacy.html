NINE & WEIRD · PLAYER PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Nine & Weird is a symbol-based Sudoku puzzle game published through the developer's GitHub Pages site. This policy explains what information the game uses, where it is stored, and what choices players have.

1. Information the game uses

  • Account information: If you register with email and password, Supabase Authentication handles your email address and login credentials. The game does not store your password in its player-profile table.
  • Cloud guest accounts: If you choose Cloud Guest, Supabase can create an anonymous user identifier without asking for your email address. The game assigns a display name and allows you to choose an avatar.
  • Profile details: The game stores the display name and avatar you choose in Supabase for signed-in or cloud-guest accounts.
  • Gameplay data: At the current stage, puzzle history and performance measurements, favorites, and points are saved in your browser on this device. Cloud synchronization of this gameplay data is being developed and is not yet active.
  • Technical/security information: Authentication and anti-bot systems may process technical information such as device, browser, network, or IP information as needed to operate and protect their services.

2. Google and other social sign-in

Google sign-in is being configured and is not yet available as a working option in the game. If enabled, it will use Google's sign-in process through Supabase to receive the account identity information needed to authenticate you, typically your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile picture. The game does not ask for access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or contacts.

Other social login providers may be added later. This policy will be updated before any additional data uses are introduced.

3. Why we use this information

  • To create and maintain player accounts and cloud guest identities.
  • To display and save usernames and avatars.
  • To maintain puzzle history and preferences on your device.
  • To protect account registration from abuse and automated traffic.
  • To troubleshoot technical issues and maintain the game.

Proposed features such as public leaderboards, Battle Mode, paid tickets, and advertisement rewards are not yet operational in this version of the game.

4. Services that help operate the game

  • Supabase: Authentication, account sessions, and cloud profile information. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Helps distinguish automated traffic from genuine users during account operations. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts the game's website and assets. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google: If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose it, Google processes the login step. Google privacy policy.

5. Browser storage and account sessions

The game uses browser storage to keep your local preferences, puzzle activity, and points. Supabase also stores authentication session information on your device so you can stay signed in. Clearing site data or using another device may remove local progress.

Cloud Guest accounts are not automatically recoverable on another device. Until account linking is implemented, signing out or losing the saved session may permanently remove access to the anonymous account's cloud profile.

6. Advertising and purchases

The current code examined for this policy does not include an operational ad network or in-game purchase flow. If advertising, analytics, or purchases are introduced, this policy will be revised to explain the relevant data practices before those features are released.

7. Sharing and retention

Information is provided to the service providers above as needed to operate the game. We do not currently use game account information to sell targeted advertising audiences.

Local puzzle data stays in your browser until you remove it or the browser clears it. Account and profile records remain in Supabase until deleted or otherwise removed under applicable retention and backup practices. A self-service account deletion feature has not yet been implemented.

8. Your choices and requests

You can play without creating a permanent email account, choose a display name and avatar, or stop using the game. You can also clear local game data through your browser's site-data controls (this may permanently remove local progress).

For questions about account information, access, correction, or deletion, contact the developer using the contact route below. Please do not post passwords, tokens, private email addresses, or other sensitive details in a public GitHub issue.

9. Contact the developer

Nine & Weird is maintained by the developer of the Nine & Weird GitHub project. To request privacy help, you can open a support issue (if the repository has Issues enabled) requesting a private way to contact the developer. Do not include private account information in a public issue.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this page when game features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
