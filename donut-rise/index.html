THE RULES

Each connected strip uses one donut design so you can tell blocks apart. The board is 8 columns wide and 10 rows tall. Slide a connected group of 1–4 donuts left or right within its existing row; you cannot lift or manually drop it. You cannot jump over other groups. After each successful slide, the whole group falls automatically as far as it can without splitting. Completed 8-donut rows clear for 100 points, plus +50 for each marked donut in the cleared row. Cascades earn combo multipliers. Every successful slide raises one new row from the bottom; there is no timer. The game ends if the stack crosses the top.

You can drag a group or tap a group and then tap an empty cell in the same row.
